## Interpretation

The word-15 cap blocks the previous F802 HW85 bridge and does not expose an
alternate lower-HW path. This agrees with F812: the F800 branch can reach HW85
only by over-filling into the word-15-heavy pattern, and that basin is locally
closed.

The useful next direction is no longer capping this exact bridge. We need a
selector that prefers F788-like balanced add/remove transitions before the
branch reaches F800/F802, likely during the original F799 motif-gated sampling.
