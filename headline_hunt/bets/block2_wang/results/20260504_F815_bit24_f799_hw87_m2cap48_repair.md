## Interpretation

M2 weight 48 is still not enough to repair the F799 HW87 detour. The uncapped
F800 repair reaches HW86 at M2 weight 50, while caps 44 and 48 both stay at
HW87.

```text
max M2 weight 44: stays HW87
max M2 weight 48: stays HW87
uncapped/weight 50: reaches HW86, then over-filled branch closes
```

This makes the threshold very sharp. The F799 branch needs to jump to at least
M2 weight 50 to improve, and that jump appears to put it on the wrong side of
the successful F788/F789 transition geometry.
