## Interpretation

The F800 HW86 repair depends on leaving the F788/F789 M2-weight band. With
M2 weight capped at 44, the F799 HW87 detour cannot improve at all.

This is an important boundary:

```text
F799 HW87, uncapped pure-HW repair -> F800 HW86 at M2 weight 50
F799 HW87, max M2 weight 44      -> stays HW87
```

The successful F788->F789 path is not just "low M2 weight." It has a specific
balanced add/remove transition that allows HW to collapse while staying sparse.
The F799 branch needs a much larger M2-weight jump to move at all, and that
jump leads into a closed over-filled basin.
