## Interpretation

This is a small positive for the detour-then-repair idea. F766 showed that the
F765 HW88 target detour was not repaired by pure-HW pair-beam. F769 shows that
the F768 HW85 detour from the looser F760 basin can be repaired to HW84.

The repair step gives up much of the target-lane improvement:

```text
F768 seed: HW85, target L1 13, c/g 129
F769 best: HW84, target L1 22, c/g 128
```

So the beam is repairing HW by drifting away from the lane target. Still, this
opens a concrete side basin below the sampled HW85 detour. It should be mapped
once before abandoning this branch.
