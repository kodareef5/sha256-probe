## Interpretation

The cap did exactly what it was meant to do: it blocked the over-filled
M2-weight-56 F802 HW85 endpoint. But it did not reveal an alternate lower-HW
bridge under the same pair pool.

This narrows the hypothesis. The F800->F802 bridge needs those extra added
bits to reach HW85, but that over-filled basin is locally closed. A simple
M2-weight cap is too blunt; the next useful version would constrain *where*
the additions occur, especially avoiding the word-15-heavy pattern seen in
F800->F802.
