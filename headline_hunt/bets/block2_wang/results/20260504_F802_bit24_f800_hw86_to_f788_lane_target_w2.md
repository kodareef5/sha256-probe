## Interpretation

This is the most useful post-F789 idea so far. Pure-HW continuation from the
F800 HW86 point closed in F801, but a target-lane bridge toward the successful
F788 HW86 shape found a new HW85 witness.

The new witness is still above the global HW78 floor, but it proves lane-shape
bridging can move a closed side basin into a lower-HW basin. That is a different
operator from the direct overlap selector and should be explored before more
blind overlap samples.
