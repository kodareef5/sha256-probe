## Interpretation

The late-word motif is real but insufficient. It focuses the broad sample by
about half, but still finds no HW82 tie:

```text
F776 broad sample: 19.97M evaluated, 0 HW82 ties
F781 late3 sample: 10.62M evaluated, 0 HW82 ties
```

The successful F760/F763 compositions are not explained simply by "many late
word pairs." The compatibility rule must include more structure, likely the
specific pairing between late words and early/mid repair words, or a nonlinear
carry-cancellation signature not captured by standalone lane-delta sums.
