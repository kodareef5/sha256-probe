## Interpretation

The F768 HW85 detour is much less flexible than the original F760 HW82 basin.
F777 has zero one-pair target improvements, and F778 has zero target
improvements through six atlas-beam depths.

This also explains why constrained target repair was brittle in F773-F775. The
local target-lane coordinate around the F768 HW85 detour is not easy to improve
or preserve while repairing HW.

The generic HW pair-beam F769 did find HW84 from this seed, but F778 could not
reproduce it using the atlas-selected target/repair pair pool. That means the
productive F769 repair uses pair primitives outside the local atlas buckets
chosen here.
