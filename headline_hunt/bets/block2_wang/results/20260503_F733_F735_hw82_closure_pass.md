## Verdict

HW82 survives the first restart pass under:

- pure total-HW ranking,
- the same c/g ranking that found HW82 from HW85,
- and a c/g/h weighted-lane objective.

This is not a proof of closure. It says the immediate radius-12 pair-beam
neighborhood around the HW82 witness does not continue the F732 descent under
these three simple objectives.
