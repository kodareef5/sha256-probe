**Kill_criterion:** "constants don't compose, OR de58 fibre ≠ 2^hw(db56)." — **fired? no.** Constants compose to identity; the fibre equals 2^hw(db56) exactly.

**Verdict reasoning:** SURVIVES by the letter of the kill (both clauses pass), but this is a **rename/restatement, not a derivation** — the #5 question answered NO. (i) The "lone non-trivial fibre" picture is correct and reproduces (de57/59/60 are identity deltas that compose trivially; de58 is the unique non-identity image) — but that is exactly QI3's localization, already known. (ii) The card's load-bearing promise, that *functoriality DERIVES* |de58|, fails twice: the de58 fibre is **not a subgroup/coset**, so additive structure cannot produce the exponent hw(db56); and de58 does not even satisfy the additive composition law (self-inverse sum ≠ 0). The genuine origin of 2^hw(db56) remains the NT3 Maj/AND-image count on db56's bits — a carry nonlinearity — which the delta-lens neither predicts nor reconstructs. So the framing relabels de58 as "the lone fibre" without computing the one number (the exponent) that would make it a mechanism. Not CONFIRMED.

Skeptic caveat: a charitable reading might call constants-compose-to-identity a partial confirmation, but the card explicitly stakes its non-relabel content on "predicts which register *and how much*," and the how-much (the exponent) is not delivered.
