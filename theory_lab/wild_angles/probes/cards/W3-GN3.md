# W3-GN3 — Reachable-difference zonotope; 132 hard-core = its degenerate directions   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** modular adds are Minkowski sums of segments → output diffs form a zonotope; its *collapsed* directions (**cokernel of the generator matrix G**) = forced/hard-core bits (= the 132); vol(Z)=Σ|det| = collision density; **HW~74 = the L1-radius mode**.

**Probe run:** Built the diff-linear segment-generator matrix G of the 7-round masked-add tail. (A) LITERAL N=32 using the repo's 32-bit primitives (`lib.sha256.full_compression`): rows = output-diff flip patterns when each of the 128 free-word input bits (W[57..60]) is flipped; computed both the writeup's deterministic-control hard-core count (no input bit controls the output bit, stable over 48 random bases) AND the honest GF(2) corank of G. (B) SMALL N=8 via the scaled-N cascade tail (compiled lab-side). (C) L1-radius: HW histogram of reachable output diffs over 4000 bases. Throttled (`taskpolicy -b`, OMP=2).

**Result (numbers):**
- **Honest GF(2) corank of the generator G (the card's literal "cokernel of G"): corank(G) = 128** at N=32 (rank 128); **corank(G) = 32 = 4N** at N=8 (rank 32). A clean **0-or-4N** quantity — exactly prior finding #1's prediction. **NOT 132.**
- Deterministic-control "hard core" = **138/256** here (support {a:32, b:32, e:32, f:32} + c:10), vs the writeup's 132 (its dc count was 4). The "+scattered dc" tail is **sample-dependent (4 → 10)**, i.e. not a stable basis-independent number.
- The two notions **do not coincide**: cokernel(G)=128 (clean, exactly the 4 registers' worth of dimension); control-hardcore≈132–138 (a different, unstable statistic). The card equates them; they differ by the wandering dc count.
- "Output bits never hit by any generator column" = **0** — every output bit *is* reachable; the rank deficiency is from linear dependencies among the 128 segments mapping into 256 outputs, so the cokernel is a 128-dim subspace **without** clean per-register support (not "the {a,b,e,f} bits").
- L1-radius: reachable de63 HW mean=128.0, median=128, **mode=129**, range [101,159]. **L1-radius mode ≈ 128 (avalanche half-of-256), NOT 74.**

**Kill_criterion:** "cokernel doesn't track the hard-core fraction, or L1 mode ≠ plateau" — **fired? YES, on both clauses.** corank(G)=128 (tracks 4N), not the 132 hard-core control-count; and the L1-radius mode is 128, not the 74 plateau.

**Verdict reasoning:** This is the **132 category error** (prior finding #1, now hit a 6th time), caught quantitatively. The card's actual linear object — the cokernel/corank of the zonotope generator matrix G — is **128** (a clean 0-or-4N dimension: the four registers a,b,e,f worth of output coordinates the 128 free-word segments cannot independently reach), not a stable 132. The "132" is a *different* object: a deterministic-control sensitivity count (#output bits no single input bit flips with prob 1), which is sample-dependent (138 here, 132 in the writeup; its "+4 dc" piece wandered to +10) and is **not** a basis-independent corank. The card conflates a control statistic with a linear cokernel and asserts they equal 132 — they don't (128 ≠ 138, and neither is stably 132). The L1-radius sub-claim fails too: the reachable-difference zonotope's HW mode is 128 (full avalanche), while 74 is a *constrained-search* plateau (the 132 quasi-random bits surviving cascade constraints), a wholly different quantity. Per the card's own bar ("NEVER CONFIRM a near-132 without a real, stable, basis-independent corank with {a,b,e,f}+4dc support"): no such object exists here. KILLED.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The honest corank is robust: it is exactly 4N at both N=8 (32) and N=32 (128), basis-independent (Gaussian elimination over GF(2)), and matches finding #1's "0 or 128" exactly. The control hard-core's instability is the tell — re-running with a different base/seed moves the dc count, which a genuine linear invariant could not do. One charitable angle: the **integer/real** zonotope (carry-bearing, not XOR-linearized) could in principle have a larger degenerate set — but the card's "Minkowski sum of segments → cokernel" is inherently the linearized generator I built, and the modular/carry view (de58 finding) does not produce a stable 132 corank either (it produces the 4N-dim forced subspace). vol(Z) vs 260 was not separately enumerated because the corank and L1 clauses already fire the kill; a full real-zonotope volume is a much larger build (REBUILD-territory) and is not needed to reject the stated 132 identification.
