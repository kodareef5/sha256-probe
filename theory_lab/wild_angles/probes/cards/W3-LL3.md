# W3-LL3 — Lopsided/cluster-expansion → derives 2^-2N as a Shearer sign change   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** The cascade's NEGATIVE round-correlations put it in the lopsided regime; the independent-set (Shearer) polynomial Z's sign = existence. The two-condition round SQUARES a vertex weight (p→p²≈2^-2N), flipping Z through zero at exactly sr=61.

**Probe run:** N=8,10, throttled (OMP=2, taskpolicy -b), 4000 conditioned samples each (msg-2 = MSB cascade kernel). (A) Measured per-round bad-event probabilities p[r]=Pr[de(r)≠0] and tap-linked pairwise joints Pr[B_i∧B_j] vs p_i·p_j, reporting the relative deviation against the 1/√samples sampling-noise floor. **Result (numbers):**
- **No negative correlation.** All 5 tap-linked pairs: relative deviation (joint−p_ip_j)/p_ip_j = **−1e-5 to −1e-6**, against sampling noise ≈0.016 — every pair is **NULL (indistinguishable from 0)**. neg=0, pos=0, null=5 at both N. The lopsided premise is unmet; the bad events are effectively independent (consistent with the verified independence ratio 1.005).
- **Z NEVER flips sign.** Single-weight vs squared-weight sr=61 vertex: Z stays **positive and barely moves** (N=10: 0.9951→0.9961; N=8: 0.9806→0.9844). Over the whole base-weight sweep, **squaring the vertex never crosses Z through 0** (flip=False for every p). At p=0.5 Z=0 for both (degeneracy), unmoved by squaring.
- **The mechanism runs backwards:** squaring a vertex weight makes it *smaller*, which makes Z (negative-weight independent-set polynomial) *larger* — moving it AWAY from zero, the opposite of the claimed "flip through zero."
- Truncation-STABLE (sign identical on window 5 and 7). Rank-2 g2=g1+h re-confirmed True on data — but that is the KNOWN structure (NT4/RG1-B), not produced by the Shearer polynomial.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if positive correlations (wrong regime), or Z's sign is truncation-dependent." — **fired? literally no** (correlations are null not positive; Z is truncation-stable) — **but the card's affirmative mechanism is decisively absent.**
