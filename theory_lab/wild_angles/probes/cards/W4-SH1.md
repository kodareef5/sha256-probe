# W4-SH1 — Sheaf-Laplacian kernel = collision count; spectral-gap collapse = the boundary   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** dim ker(L=δᵀδ) = harmonic difference-sections = the linear collision space; λ₁ = energy of the least-inconsistent non-collision, conjectured to collapse adding round 61. Headline: ker dim **equals** the collision count.

**Probe run:** built the cellular sheaf the whole wave presupposes (`kernels/sheaf_delta.py`): stalks = GF(2)^N register-diff words a..h over the unrolled tail; δ = linearized round relations (Σ/σ EXACT over GF(2), Ch/Maj = base-point GF(2) Jacobian, modular add → XOR / carries dropped); L = δᵀδ (real incidence) for `eigvalsh`. Two clauses scored separately. (A) equality: linear-sheaf nullity vs the established brute-force sr=60 counts {N=4:49, 8:260, 10:946, 12:2955}, matched free-set (input kernel word + schedule diffs). (B) gap: λ₁ of L swept over tail-depth R=1..5 (sr 57→61), N=4, 3 seeds. Throttled (OMP=2, taskpolicy -b).

**Result (numbers):**
- **[A — EQUALITY] FAILS.** With the free-set matched to the modular collision space (da + schedule diffs, db..dh pinned), dim ker(L) = **0** at N=4,8,10,12 (R=7) → 2⁰ = **1 collision predicted**, vs established counts **49 / 260 / 946 / 2955** (log₂ = 5.6 / 8.0 / 9.9 / 11.5). dim ker(L) ≠ log₂(count) at every N. Structurally it *cannot* match: a GF(2)-subspace dimension is an integer, while log₂(count) is non-integer and grows ~0.74–0.96·N. (Un-pinned, the linear nullity is R·N — wrong R-dependence, far too big — the admitted overcount; pinned, it collapses to 0. Neither value is the count.)
- **[B — GAP COLLAPSE] FAILS.** λ₁(R) at N=4 = 0.076 → 0.0011 → 0.0015 → 0.137 → 0.195 (sr 57→61). At the **60→61 step (R=4→5)** the ratio λ₁(prev)/λ₁ = **0.70** (and 0.38, 0.75 on seeds 111, 999) — λ₁ **increases**, no collapse. The only large drops are at R=1→2 and are wildly seed-dependent (70×, 10×, 308×) = noise, not a knee. No ≥2× collapse localized at the boundary.

**Kill_criterion:** "ker doesn't track the count, or λ₁ doesn't collapse (2×)." — **fired? YES (both clauses).**

**Verdict reasoning:** KILLED on both halves. The headline equality "sheaf-Laplacian kernel = collision count" is false as a *number*: the linear kernel dimension is 0 (or an integer multiple of N), never the saturating non-integer log₂(count) — answering prior-finding #5's testable question directly: ker dim does **not** equal the collision count, it is at best a fitted analogy (and a bad one — off by 5.6–11.5 bits). And per prior-finding #4 there is no round-60 knee: λ₁ does not collapse at 60→61 (it rises), with the only large gaps appearing early and seed-erratic. The carry nonlinearity that makes the real count grow as 2^{0.74N} is exactly what the linear sheaf throws away, so its harmonic subspace carries none of the count.

No — clause A's obstruction is dimensional (integer vs non-integer-times-N, verified at four N), independent of base point. Clause B was confirmed stable across 3 seeds (ratio at 60→61 always <1). The early-R drops being seed-noise is itself the tell that L's small eigenvalues are conditioning artifacts, not a Cheeger gap. Independent corroboration: matches the wave-wide "no round-60 knee (~8×)" and the IG-line finding that linear/metric reductions of this object are full-rank or zero-rank, never 132/0.74. The skeptic's "test predictiveness not equality" out is unavailable here because the predicted number (count) and the gap location (60) both fail.
