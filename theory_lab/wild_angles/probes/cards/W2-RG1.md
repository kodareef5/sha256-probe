# W2-RG1 — Maxwell–Calladine isostatic point → "why round 60" AND codim-2 → 2^-2N   ·   VERDICT: MIXED — clause A **KILLED**, clause B **CONFIRMED**

**Card claim:** The sr-boundary is where the collision framework becomes isostatic — floppy(r)=D(r)−C(r) crosses zero at r≈60 (Maxwell–Calladine), and each round past isostatic adds exactly **two** constraints (the g1=0 AND h=0 pair) ⇒ codim-2 ⇒ 2^-2N.

**Probe run:** N=10, throttled (OMP=2, taskpolicy -b). Reused shabridge SHA primitives via a width-parameterized mini-SHA (validated against the repo's cascade construction). **Clause A:** built a *genuine* sr=60 cascade-DP collision (msg-2 tail words via the repo's `find_w2`, then brute-solved w60 for de61=0 — the repo's actual sr=60 object, free4=[436,191,389,0], **GENUINE-collision=True**). Re-ran at 5 random points. **Clause B:** from the 946 N=10 collisions in `gap_rows.csv`, tested the rank-2 relation g2=g1+h and the GF(2) rank of the sr61 constraint system {g1=0, g2=0}.

**Result (numbers):**

(The increment profile *is* genuinely different from the XOR-linear W1-PH2 result of flat [10,10,10,10] — so carries matter — but it still does not vindicate either card prediction.)
- **Clause B — codim-2 confirmed exactly.** g2 == (g1+h) mod 2^N for **946/946** collisions (the rank-2 survivor). The sr61 step {g1=0, g2=0} ⇔ {g1=0, h=0} on disjoint N-bit blocks; GF(2) rank of the 20×20 constraint matrix = **20 = 2 words = codim-2**. Whole-population independence ratio (repo, 1B samples) = **1.005** ⇒ g1 ⊥ h ⇒ each enforced round costs 2^-2N.
