# W1-DY2 — Bowen pressure -> the sr-cliff as a phase transition   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** Assign each per-round transition a potential phi = log(realization prob); pressure P(beta) = lim (1/n) log sum_orbits exp(beta sum phi). Bowen's equation: collision rate = beta* solving P(beta*)=0. The sr=60->61 cliff is a non-analytic KINK in P(beta) (losing W[60]-freedom sends one transition's phi -> -inf), and 2^-2N is the potential at the boundary.

**Probe run:** Reused the DY1 differential transfer operator (kernels/transfer_operator.py) with weights = realization probabilities, formed L_beta with entries p_ij^beta, traced P(beta)=log2 lambda_max(L_beta) over a 60-point beta-grid at N=6,8, in both the W60-FREE regime (message difference free) and the W60-FIXED regime (msgdiff=0, cascade / sr=61 side). Detected kinks via max |d2P/dbeta2| and located the Bowen root P(beta*)=0. Throttled, ~7 s/N. Ran a follow-up diagnostic on the fixed-regime matrix and its eigenvalue trajectory.

**Result (numbers):**
- P(1): FIXED = +0.0000 (exactly), FREE = -3.82 (N=6) / -8.68 (N=8). The stochastic operator pins P(1)=0 identically.
- Bowen root beta*: fixed = 0.70 (N=6) / 0.40 (N=8); free = 0.64 / 0.31. These are NOT a rate; for a proper stochastic operator beta* is identically 1 (P(1)=0 always), so the Bowen construction carries zero information about 0.74.
- "Kink": fixed-regime max |d2P/dbeta2| = 47-49, located at beta~0.6. DIAGNOSTIC shows why: the fixed (msgdiff=0) operator is degenerate — column (0,0) sums to 1.0 with self-loop prob 1.0, but every other column sums to only 0.03-0.09 (the nonzero-head mass escapes the tracked set). The Perron eigenvalue is 76.5 at beta=0.1 (driven by the leaky sub-1 entries raised to a small power) and collapses to exactly 1.000 for beta>~0.8 (the trivial (0,0) self-loop taking over). The "kink" is precisely this crossover between a leaky-transient eigenvalue and the constant self-loop eigenvalue — a generic linear-algebra artifact, NOT tied to W[60]-freedom or any sr-cliff.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if P(beta) is smooth across the round-60 fix/free switch (no kink), or beta*-rate disagrees >10%." — **fired? YES.**

**Verdict reasoning:** The second clause fires outright: the Bowen root is beta*=1 for any probability-weighted operator (P(1)=log2(1)=0 always), so beta* does not reproduce the 0.74 rate — it is structurally independent of the dynamics. (The apparent beta*~0.7 is an artifact of leaky column normalization, and its numerical proximity to 0.74 is a coincidence of the leak, not a Bowen identity.) The first clause also effectively fires: the only non-analyticity present is a numerical eigenvalue-crossover between a leaky-transient mode and the trivial self-loop, which appears regardless of the fix/free switch and is not a thermodynamic phase transition. The card's own skeptic line — "SHA has fixed n=64; the transition could be a finite-size crossover" — is exactly what the data shows.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** A genuine first-order phase transition needs a Perron-eigenvalue degeneracy (two analytic branches crossing) in an n->infinity limit; the SHA operator is finite (n=64 rounds) and the observed kink is a 2-eigenvalue crossover whose location (beta~0.6) and existence are governed by the matrix's leak structure, not by removing the W[60] transition. The recurrent-interior version (a properly normalized, non-leaky weighted operator over rounds 16-59 with a real potential) might still admit a non-trivial pressure curve — but as specified (weights = probabilities, rate = beta* root, kink = sr-cliff), the construction is vacuous (beta*=1) and the kink is artifactual. Shares the DY1 finding that the differential operator is not low-rank under generic conditioning (300-head cap hit; columns leak).
