# W2-PC1 — Boundary-expansion width jump → resolution lower bound at 61   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** Build the sr=k collision-constraint bipartite graph G_k (k=58..61); (a) Hopcroft-Karp — does G_61 FAIL the constraint→private-variable matching while G_60 PASSES? (b) min boundary δ(s)/s with a *constant lower bound only at 61* = the headline (Ben-Sasson–Wigderson width ≥ Ω(c·s)).

**Probe run:** Built G_k from the exact GF(2)-linearized SHA-256 message-schedule support of the free words W[57..60] (carry-monotone, so edges are UNDER-counted = hardest case for the attacker's matching = most generous to the card). LEFT = de(r)=0 collision constraints for enforced rounds 57..k at bit granularity; RIGHT = the 4N free message bits. Ran Kuhn max-matching + full-set boundary (unique-neighbour) ratio per round, N=4,6,8, two constraint accountings (the verified 2-conditions/round, and the naive 1-condition/round). Throttled.

**Result (numbers):** Measured the schedule fan-in directly: rounds 57/58/59/60 each get one *fresh* free word (57/58/59/60); rounds 61/62/63 RE-USE W59/W60/W59 via the recurrence (W54,W46,W45 are fixed block-1 words) — so matchability is governed purely by free-word re-use.
- **Faithful 2-condition model (the verified rank-2 g1=0 ∧ h=0, 2N bit-constr/round):** matching fails from **sr=57** onward (2N constraints vs N free bits per governing word). Boundary ratio **flat = 0.000 for every round 57→63** (per-step Δ all 0.000). KILL CLAUSE 1 fires.
- **Naive 1-condition model (N constr/round):** matching feasible through sr=60, fails at sr=61 — *superficially* the card's pattern — BUT this is pure free-word counting (4 words run out after round 60). The **boundary ratio is FLAT at exactly 1/N for rounds 57–60** (0.250 / 0.167 / 0.125 at N=4/6/8) and then **DECAYS smoothly** after 61 (N=8: 0.125,0.125,0.125,0.125 → 0.075 → 0.042 → 0.036). It goes the **wrong direction** (down) and shows **no discontinuous jump to a constant floor at 61**.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if G_60 already fails the matching (also over-determined yet easy), OR a bounded-size UNSAT sub-core exists at sr=61." — **fired? YES (clause 1).** In the faithful 2-condition accounting the matching is over-determined from sr=57; and every round-block is itself a bounded UNSAT sub-core (a single round already has more bit-conditions than its governing word's free bits).

**Verdict reasoning:** KILLED. The card needs the *boundary expansion* to JUMP to a constant lower bound exactly at 61. It does not: δ(s)/s is **flat across all rounds** (0 in the faithful model; 1/N then smoothly decaying in the naive model), never a discontinuous upward step at 61. The only thing that flips at 61 is matchability, and that flip is exactly the boundary-proof's slack=−64 free-word bookkeeping (rounds 61–63 re-use words 59/60) — *not* a Ben-Sasson–Wigderson expansion phase transition. This is lead finding #4 realized at the proof-complexity level: the per-round quantity is smooth, the "61 jump" is relabeled word-counting, and (per #1) the obstruction is the carry nonlinearity (which this linear incidence graph deliberately cannot see), not a surviving expanding core.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** A clean negative. Skeptic angle: maybe the *real* (carry-coupled) incidence graph has more edges and a genuine expander hides there — but more edges only make matching EASIER and boundary expansion SMALLER (denser → fewer unique neighbours), so the conservative under-counted graph here is the most expander-friendly case and it still shows no jump. Corroborates W1-PH2 / W2-CT2 (smooth control-dimension decay, no knee) and the rank-2 ground truth (PH1/IN3): enforcing a round costs *two* conditions uniformly, with no special expansion event at 61. To rescue any version of this one would need a carry-DAG Tseitin graph with measured expansion — which is exactly what W2-PC5 probes (and finds is non-expanding).
