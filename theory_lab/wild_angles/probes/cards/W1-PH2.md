# W1-PH2 — RG fixed point → explains "why round 60"   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** The 64-round iteration is an RG flow; the boundary (round ~60) is where the controllable difference-operator turns irrelevant — the largest eigenvalue of the linearized difference-propagation Jacobian crosses 1, and that eigenvalue is set by the rotation constants, so the boundary should be *computable*. A knee in control-dimension-vs-round should sit near the wall and *move* when rotations are permuted.

**Probe run:** N=8,10,12. Built the 8N×8N GF(2) XOR-linearized round matrix M (kernel `linround.py`); injected each free tail word W[i] (i=57..63) into T1→(a',e'), propagated to the output (round 63) via M, and measured dim R(r) = dimension of output difference-directions controllable using free words from round r onward. Located the knee (largest per-round control drop) and re-ran under 5 rotation-constant variants. Throttled (OMP=2, taskpolicy -b).

**Result (numbers):**
- Control dimension decays **perfectly linearly** with NO knee. N=10: dim R = 70, 60, 50, 40, 30, 20, 10 for r = 57…63. Every round removes **exactly N** controllable directions (N=8: steps of 8; N=12: steps of 12). All six per-round drops are identical (10,10,10,10,10,10 at N=10) — a flat decline, no cliff at round 60 or anywhere.
- **Rotation permutation has zero effect.** dim R(57)=70 and knee position r=58 are *identical* across SHA rotations, S0↔S1 swap, all-equal (1,1,1), spread, and even identity-rotation (0,0,0). Distinct knee positions across all variants: {58}. Distinct dim R(57): {70}.
- Adding a Ch/Maj linear surrogate (richer linear model) gives the **same** 70→60→…→10. Spectral radius of the real-lifted round matrix is ρ≈3.30 (SHA) / 1.65 (identity-rot): always **>1 and constant across rounds** (one fixed matrix) — no "eigenvalue crosses 1 at round 60" event.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if the controllable dimension decays smoothly (no knee), or if permuting rotations doesn't move the knee." — **fired? yes (BOTH clauses)**

**Verdict reasoning:** Both kill clauses fire decisively. (1) The control dimension decays *smoothly* — exactly linearly, N directions per round, with no knee at the boundary. (2) Permuting the rotation constants (even to degenerate/identity) does not move the (non-existent) knee at all. Under XOR-linearization a rotation is just a bit-permutation composed with XOR, which preserves GF(2) rank, so each free word contributes a full N independent controllable directions regardless of the constants — there is simply no structure for an RG boundary to live in. This is exactly the card's own skeptic warning realized: *"XOR-linearization may wash out the carry nonlinearity that sets the boundary."* The repo's ground truth corroborates the diagnosis — the 132 hard-core bits arise from **carry chains and the T1+T2 modular nonlinearity**, none of which survives linearization. The RG/eigenvalue-crossing picture cannot explain "why round 60" *through a linear transfer operator*; the boundary is a carry phenomenon. KILLED (as stated).

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** A clean negative, not softened. Could the linear model be too impoverished to *deserve* the name "the Jacobian"? Yes — and that is precisely the point: the card stakes its claim on the *linearized* Jacobian's eigenvalue, and that object is uniformly expanding (ρ≈3.3 every round) with no threshold crossing. To rescue a "why round 60" RG story one would need a model that retains carries (e.g. the Lipmaa-Moriai carry-DAG or a 2-adic/p-adic linearization) — a fundamentally different, non-XOR object. Within the card's stated probe, the answer is unambiguous. Independent corroboration: the perfectly-quantized N-per-round decay reproduces the trivial "free-word counting" of the boundary proof (4 free words at sr=60), confirming the linear model sees only the message-freedom bookkeeping, never the carry wall.
