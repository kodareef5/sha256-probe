# W8-WE1 — Weihrauch product jump: sr=60 is C_{2^N}, sr=61 is C_{2^N}⋆C_{2^N}   ·   VERDICT: SURVIVES (rename only — NOT a mechanism)

**Card claim:** sr≤60 = a single bounded closed-choice (cascade = the reduction); sr=61's g1=0 ∧ h=0 = a *parallel product* of two independent choices; the gating-condition count Q(r) steps 1→2 at 61 (and advice A:0→2N, arity K:1→2 agree). The Weihrauch degree should *derive/forecast* the higher rounds (e.g. sr=62).

**Probe run:** Instrumented the repo's exact cascade-DP tail (`lib.sha256` via shabridge; pure-Python mirror of `gap_analysis.c`) to (a) count independent gating conditions Q(r) per round with the memo's independence ratio test, and (b) — adversarially — push one round further and measure the de-filter ladder over the FULL tuple space (Thm3: full collision = de61=0 ∧ de62=0 ∧ de63=0) to test whether the per-round arity stays 2 (forecasting sr=62=2^-4N). N=5 (Python, exact, throttled) + N=8 (C enumerator over 2^32 = 4.29·10⁹ tuples, throttled). The C run reproduces the repo's 260-collision ground truth exactly.

**Result (numbers):**
- **Q(57..60) = 0** — cascade invariant da=0 forced for BOTH messages at every free round (find_w2 solves W2); de60 auto-zero (Thm2). No coincidence ⇒ Q=0. (N=5: da≠0 count = 0 at r=57,58,59,60; de60≠0 count = 0.)
- **Q(61) = 2** — g1=0 (per-message value match) ⊥ h=0 (inter-message compat). Independence ratio **1.047** (N=5), **0.923** (N=8) — both ≈1 ⇒ two independent N-bit conditions. Matches the established 2^-2N headline exactly.
- **62-step arity = 1, NOT 2** (the adversarial test). de-filter ladder at N=8 over 4.29·10⁹ tuples:
  - de61=0: P=0.003775, **round-61 e-step exponent 1.006**
  - de62=0 | de61: P=0.003991, **round-62 e-step exponent 0.996**
  - de63=0 | de61&de62: P=0.004018, **round-63 e-step exponent 0.995**
  - FULL (de61&de62&de63) = **260 collisions, total exponent 2.997 ≈ 2^-3N** (one e-condition per round).
  - Per-message value-match analog at 61 (does W1[61] equal its own schedule?) = **1.000** (16 211 828/16 211 828): W[61] is schedule-FORCED, so there is NO second free per-message lever at round 62 — only the e-filter remains. (N=5 corroborates: P(de62=0|de61=0)=0.0309, exponent 1.003.)

**Kill_criterion:** "Q≥2 at some r≤60, or the two conditions are dependent at 61 (collapses to 1), or Q flat/smooth." — **fired? NO.** Q=0 for r≤60, Q=2 at 61, the two are independent (ratio≈1), and Q is not flat (0→2 step at 61).

**Verdict reasoning:** The kill_criterion does not fire, so the angle SURVIVES — the parallel-product *picture* (sr=61 = two independent choices) is a faithful relabelling of the measured g1⊥h structure. But it is explicitly **NOT a CONFIRMED mechanism**: the card's only would-be-novel content over the established 2^-2N is a *derivation/forecast* from the Weihrauch degree, and the natural reading "C^⋆2 per enforced round" forecasts sr=62 = 2^-4N (full = 2^-6N). The data **refute that forecast**: each enforced round adds exactly ONE ~2^-N e-condition (exponents 1.006/0.996/0.995), so sr=62-from-sr60 = 2^-3N and the full collision = 2^-3N (260 at N=8), not 2^-6N. The "two conditions" at 61 are a one-time event — converting the last *free* word W[60] into a schedule-matched word (per-message value g1) **plus** the e-filter — not a per-round doubling; rounds 62/63 have no free word left to convert, so they cost one filter each. Therefore WE1 = **rename of g1,h at the single 61 boundary**, and the parallel-product arithmetic does NOT correctly predict sr=62. Per the sweep's "a rename is not a CONFIRMED" rule, this is logged SURVIVES (rename), not CONFIRMED.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The 2^-2N headline and the 2^-3N full-collision figure are NOT in tension — they are different conditionings. 2^-2N = P(an *sr=60 collision* is also sr=61) = g1=0 ∧ h=0 (the freedom was already "spent" by the cascade construction). 2^-3N = P(a *raw tuple* is a full collision) = de61=0 ∧ de62=0 ∧ de63=0 (three independent e-filters). My N=8 run reproduces BOTH (independence ratio 0.923 for the former; ladder exponents ≈1 each and 260 collisions for the latter), from the same enumeration — so the reconciliation is internally consistent, not a fudge. Could the 62-step secretly be 2^-2N at larger N (carry effects)? The exponent is already 0.996 at N=8 over 4.29·10⁹ tuples and 1.003 at N=5 (independent rotation scaling) — two well-separated N both give 1.0, so a jump to 2.0 at large N would be a surprise demanding its own evidence. The honest residual: I did not exhibit an actual sr=62 *collision* (it's 2^-3N rare; 0 at these N), so the forecast refutation rests on the conditional de-filter exponents, which is the same evidence class the repo used for 2^-2N.
