# W8-CL2 — c-vectors → g1⊥h is rank-2 sign-coherence; predicts sr=62 = 2^-4N   ·   VERDICT: SURVIVES (as rename)

**Card claim:** The two sr=61 conditions are a (g-vector, c-vector) pair; their verified independence (ratio 1.005) = c-vectors non-parallel (rank-2 span) = 2^-2N codim; sign-coherence forbids the collision (no lucky 2^-N candidate). Predict rank{g1,h,g1',h'}=4 ⇒ sr=62 = 2^-4N.

**Probe run:** Faithful mini-SHA(N) cascade extended to round 61 (reusing the W7-CG3 sr=62 method). N=8/10/12. (TEST 1+2) measured the four sr=62 gap marginals g1_60,h_60,g1_61,h_61, the sr=61 joint P(g1_60=0 ∧ h_60=0) and its independence ratio, and log₂(product of 4 marginals) (= the 3N-vs-4N discriminator). (TEST 3, the earn-your-keep test) measured **sign-coherence**: the per-coordinate monotonicity (signfrac = fraction of unit-steps increasing the value) of g1,h in each free word — a genuine c-vector is sign-coherent (one-signed); a generic modular functional is mixed-sign. Throttled: yes (800k/400k/200k samples).

**Result (numbers):**
- **TEST 1 (sr=62=2^-4N):** product of 4 marginals → log₂ = **−32.04 (N=8), −40.05 (N=10), −48.23 (N=12)** = exactly **−4N**. Each marginal ≈ 2^-N (e.g. N=10: g1_60=9.93e-4, h_60=1.01e-3, g1_61=9.48e-4, h_61=9.28e-4; 2^-10=9.77e-4).
- **TEST 2 (independence):** sr=61 ratio P(g1_60=0 ∧ h_60=0)/[P·P] = **0.979 at N=8** (in the card's [0.95,1.05] band); consistent with the source's 1.005-over-1.07B. (At N=10/12 the joint sr61 event is too rare in 400k/200k samples to estimate the ratio — both-count = 0 — so N=8 + source carry the independence claim.)
- **TEST 3 (sign-coherence — decisive):** g1_60 vs w60 signfrac=1.00 only because g1_60 = w60 − sched is a **trivial linear shift**. The informative coordinates are all ≈0.50: g1_60 vs w58 = **0.49/0.50**, h_60 vs w58 = **0.48/0.56**, h_60 vs w57 = **0.47/0.48** (N=8/N=10) — **mixed-sign, non-monotone**. g1,h are NOT sign-coherent c-vectors.

**Kill_criterion:** "rank <4 at any cascade candidate, OR any candidate with ratio ≳2 (a c-vector collision)." — **fired? no** (rank-4 holds → 2^-4N; ratio 0.979, never ≳2).

**Verdict reasoning:** The card's **forward prediction sr=62 = 2^-4N is CONFIRMED-by-measurement** (and already was, by W7-CG3) — but the kill not firing is the *weak* outcome here. Per prior-finding #3 and the catalog's own caveat ("'c-vector' is unearned unless the seed-coordinate signs are one-signed"), a CONFIRMED requires the cluster structure to **add a mechanism** beyond relabeling g1,h. It does not: (a) 2^-4N is just the trivial forward product of the *already-measured* per-round 2^-2N (two conditions g1,h, independent) carried one more round — no new derivation; (b) the one genuinely distinguishing cluster-algebra property, **sign-coherence**, is *absent* — the gap functionals are mixed-sign/non-monotone (signfrac≈0.5), i.e. two **generic** independent modular functionals, whose independence (rank 2) is the *default*, not a sign-coherent c-vector pair. So the cluster framing **renames** g1,h as c-vectors and re-derives a number that was already measured. **SURVIVES-as-rename, not CONFIRMED.**

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The four-marginal factorization is exactly the method the repo used to establish 2^-2N for sr=61 (1.005 over 1.07B), so the 4N measurement is on solid footing. The independence-ratio at N=10/12 is unestimable from these sample sizes (rare joint), a real limitation — but the marginals + N=8 ratio + source pin it. The sign-coherence surrogate (discrete monotonicity over Z_{2^N}) is the most faithful available stand-in for a char-0 sign; a defender could propose a different signing, but for *any* signing the measured non-monotonicity in the load-bearing coordinates blocks the "sign-coherence forbids the collision" mechanism — that lower-bound content is unearned. The 2^-4N correctness is real; the c-vector *explanation* of it is not.
