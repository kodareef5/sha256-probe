# W1-DY3 — Differential Lyapunov spectrum -> the floor as an exponent, the "2" as a 2-D unstable subspace   ·   VERDICT: SURVIVES

**Card claim:** Linearize per-round differential propagation A_r; Lyapunov exponents = log-growth of singular values of prod A_r. A collision pins the orbit to the most-contracting directions; cost/round = gap between the typical expanding and the contracting exponent. The repo's "two independent N-bit conditions" (2^-2N) = a 2-D UNSTABLE subspace => factor 2 => 2^-2N. chi_min ~ -log2 per round (two give -2N); #(chi<0) ~ cascade depth.

**Probe run:** Built the empirical 2N x 2N differential Jacobian on the (da||de) head (bit-flip sensitivity of the produced differential to a one-bit flip of the incoming differential, averaged over random conditioning states + message word, cascade regime msgdiff=0, tail-diff=0). Took a numerically-stable QR-reorthonormalized Lyapunov spectrum of the R-round product cocycle (lyapunov_qr in kernels/transfer_operator.py) at N=4,6,8,10, R=40, 4000 samples/round. Checked seed-robustness (seeds 0-4) and ran a lane-decomposition skeptic (single-lane a-only and e-only cocycles). Throttled, <1 s/N.

**Result (numbers):**
- Clear hyperbolic structure (NOT all |chi|~0): N=8 spectrum spans chi_max=+2.03 to chi_min=-7.14; N=10 spans +2.27 to -6.75.
- #strong-expanding (chi>0.3) = exactly 2 at EVERY N (4,6,8,10), STABLE across all 5 seeds. The two expanding exponents are near-degenerate (N=8: +2.03,+2.00; N=10: +2.27,+2.26) — a clean 2-D unstable subspace.
- #contracting (chi<-0.05) = 2N-2 (6,10,14,18 at N=4,6,8,10) — grows with N, NOT fixed at 2.
- chi_min ~ -3 to -7 bits/round, NOT the card's -1.0/round target.
- SKEPTIC (decisive): single-lane cocycles give exactly 1 strong-expanding mode each (lane-a #exp=1, lane-e #exp=1), and their chi_max MATCH the joint top-2 (N=8: lane-a +2.02, lane-e +1.99 vs joint +2.03,+2.00). So the "2" = one expanding mode per WRITTEN LANE (a and e) = number of adders, a structural fact of "the round updates 2 state lanes."

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if the differential Jacobian has no clear contracting directions (all |chi| ~ 0)." — **fired? NO.** There are clear contracting AND expanding directions (chi_min ~ -6, chi_max ~ +2), so the literal kill does not fire.

**Verdict reasoning:** SURVIVES, not CONFIRMED, not KILLED. The genuine, robust, seed-stable finding is that the differential cocycle IS hyperbolic — a real 2-D unstable subspace and a (2N-2)-D contracting bundle — which contradicts the naive "near-injectivity => chi~0" expectation and is the non-trivial result here. But the card's specific quantitative story does NOT confirm: chi_min is ~ -6, not the predicted ~ -1/round; the contracting count is 2N-2, not "2"; and the "2" that does appear is the UNSTABLE-subspace dimension, which the skeptic check shows is just "one expanding mode per written lane (a,e)" = #adders — a structural triviality that coincides NUMERICALLY with the sr-cliff's 2 independent conditions but is mechanistically unrelated (annihilating two chi~+2 expanding directions is not obviously the 2^-2N cost). That is coincidence, not convergence, on the "2". So the angle is alive (hyperbolicity is real and worth a deeper, geometrically-faithful probe) but the cheap probe cannot confirm the 2^-2N = 2-D-unstable-subspace mechanism.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The empirical Jacobian is bit-flip sensitivity around the zero differential, a proxy for the true tangent cocycle; the genuine modular-metric tangent map could differ (the card's own hyperbolic-toral-automaton caveat: a bijection can contract area). To CONFIRM, one would need (i) the exponents tied to the actual collision-trail directions (not generic conditioning), (ii) chi_min landing near the -log2/round target, and (iii) a derivation that annihilating the 2-D unstable subspace costs exactly 2^-2N rather than 2^-(sum of the two positive exponents). The robust "2 = #written lanes" explanation is the parsimonious reading and should be ruled out before the sr-cliff interpretation is claimed.
