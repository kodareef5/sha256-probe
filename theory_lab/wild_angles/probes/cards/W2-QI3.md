# W2-QI3 — Feed-forward monogamy → why de58 carries all the differential freedom   ·   VERDICT: SURVIVES

**Card claim:** The feed-forward "clones" internal state into the output; a monogamy inequality forces all slack into the de58 channel. Probe (N=4..10): measure corr(Δ_int,Δ_in)+corr(Δ_int,Δ_out) and per-channel slack; **predict slack≈0 in de57/59/60, all in de58.**

**Probe run:** N=4,6,8,10,12, throttled. Reproduced the repo's exact de-set measurement (`de58_enum.c`/`de60_enum.c`): a mini-SHA cascade at width N where M2 flips one kernel bit in words 0 and 9, cascade-1 correction `cw_r` is injected on path 2 each round to hold da=0, and `de_k := (e₁−e₂)` after running cascade round k (the e-register modular difference). Per held-cascade chamber I enumerate W57 fully (2^N) and collect the realized sets de57,de58,de59,de60; the per-channel "monogamy slack" S_k = log₂|de_k|. A deterministic scan picks 6 (m0,fill,bit) chambers per N where cascade-1 genuinely holds (da57=0) and seeds a nonzero difference (30 chambers total). Cross-checked each against the growth law de58 = 2^hw(db56).

**Result (numbers):** Across **all 30 held-cascade chambers, at every N**:
- |de57| = |de59| = |de60| = **1** (frozen; slack S = 0), and |de58| > 1.
- **frac_of_total_slack_in_de58 = 1.00** and **others_frozen = True** in 30/30 chambers — every bit of differential freedom localizes to de58, exactly as the card predicts.
- |de58| ranges 2…256; S58 = log₂|de58| ∈ {1,…,8} depending on chamber. Examples: N=10 m0=0x398 → |de58|=32 (S58=5); N=12 m0=0x58 → |de58|=256 (S58=8).
- The *exact* growth law de58 = 2^hw(db56) (db56 = my e-input seed diff) held only **11/30** chambers — the localization is exact, but the precise exponent depends on which "db56" seed quantity is used.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if the correlation sum has no consistent ceiling, **or slack is spread evenly across de57–60**." — **fired? NO.** Slack is the opposite of evenly spread: it is *perfectly concentrated* in de58 (de57/59/60 ≡ 1) in 30/30 chambers.

**Verdict reasoning:** SURVIVES (not CONFIRMED). The card's central, falsifiable prediction — *all* differential slack localizes to de58, with de57/59/60 frozen — reproduces exactly and robustly (30/30 chambers, N=4…12). This is a genuine, sharp localization and the kill criterion is decisively not met. I stop short of CONFIRMED because the *number* it reproduces (de57=de59=de60=1, de58 varies) is precisely the repo's known ground truth (finding #5), so this is a faithful re-derivation of the localization rather than independent corroboration of a new quantity. Crucially, the "monogamy inequality" as stated explains *which* channel holds slack but does **not by itself predict the growth law** (the de58 = 2^hw(db56) exponent) — the monogamy ledger is consistent with any |de58|, so it localizes but does not quantify. The open question (which mechanism predicts the 2^hw growth) is *not* answered by this card; monogamy gives the "where," not the "how much."

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The localization is structural, not a sampling artifact: it falls straight out of the cascade shift-register geometry — registers h,g,f at round 60 are *copies* of e at rounds 57,58,59, and the cascade-1 correction forces those e-diffs to the unique value 1 (=0 collapse) while the e-register at round 58 alone absorbs the modular-add freedom. So "monogamy" here is really the shift-register cloning the card names, and it is real. The skeptic's worry that "GF(2) monogamy may be vacuous" is half-right: the *inequality* is vacuous as a bound (it permits any de58 size), but the *localization* it predicts is non-vacuous and confirmed. A deeper probe worth promoting: derive |de58| = 2^hw(db56) from a monogamy/cloning *deficit* count — if that deficit equals hw(db56) it would upgrade this to CONFIRMED and resolve the open growth-law question. As stands, the prediction the card actually makes (de58-only slack) holds; the growth law it gestures at does not follow.
