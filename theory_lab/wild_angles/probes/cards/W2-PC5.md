# W2-PC5 — Linearization-survival (Tseitin) test → obstruction is expansion, not carries   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** XOR-linearized sr=60 still times out, yet linear systems are Gauss-easy; so the hardness is a SURVIVING Tseitin-graph EXPANSION, not nonlinearity. If L_61 is Tseitin-like over an expanding carry-incidence graph while L_59 isn't, you recover PC1's width jump with nonlinearity stripped → "the obstruction is the graph, not the carries." The scalpel experiment.

**Probe run:** Built the XOR-linearized tail collision system L_k over GF(2) (kernels/linround.py round map, carries dropped — the card's linearization), free vars = dW[57..k] bits, equations = de(k)=0 reduced over the free vars. N=4,5; k=59,60,61. Measured (0) variable-degree distribution (Tseitin faithfulness check), (1)+(2) Gaussian rank/corank + residual core, (3) residual-graph width/unique-neighbour expansion, (4) exact block-sensitivity bs(L_k). Throttled.

**Result (numbers):**
- **(0) L_k is NOT Tseitin.** Tseitin needs ~all variables at degree 2 (each var = an edge in 2 charge equations). Measured: at N=4 **every free var has degree 1** ({1:12}, {1:16}, {1:20}); at N=5 degrees are 1 and 3 — **never degree 2**. The card's own precondition ("verify genuinely Tseitin-like FIRST") fails.
- **(1)+(2) Gauss-TRIVIAL.** Full row-rank at every k (rank = #eq = N), large corank growing smoothly (8→12→16 at N=4 = +N/round). **No residual core survives elimination** — labelled "trivial/thin" at every k including 61.
- **(3) No expansion jump.** Reduced-equation avg width grows smoothly 3→4→5 (N=4) — just the support widening +1 bit/round; unique-neighbour ratio HIGH (3.0→5.0), i.e. equations barely overlap = the OPPOSITE of an expander. No constant-expansion floor appearing only at 61.
- **(4) bs(L_k) FLAT (trivially full).** bs = nvars exactly (8,12,16 / 10,15) — the linearized predicate is an affine subspace with full sensitivity; grows linearly with the free-word count, no jump at 61. (`None` at the largest k is only my 16-var truth-table cap.)

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if the residual graph at sr=61 is small/non-expanding (timeout was an encoding artifact — itself valuable), OR bs(L_k) is flat." — **fired? YES (BOTH clauses).** Residual at sr=61 is Gauss-trivial/non-expanding, AND bs is flat (= nvars, no jump).

**Verdict reasoning:** KILLED — and the thesis is **confirmed BACKWARDS**, exactly as flagged. Stripping the carries (XOR-linearization) collapses the tail system to a full-rank, immediately Gauss-solvable affine system with NO surviving expanding core and NO Tseitin structure (vars are degree 1/3, not 2). So the linearized problem is trivially easy — meaning any "XOR-linearized sr=60 timeout" is an *encoding artifact* (over-constrained-rank nonsense, exactly the card's skeptic warning), not surviving graph expansion. This positively demonstrates the obstruction was the **deleted carry nonlinearity**, not the incidence graph — vindicating finding #1 (the barrier is the carry nonlinearity) and the Wave-1 result. PC1's "width jump" does NOT survive linearization here; there is no expander to recover. The card's diagnostic, run honestly, refutes the card's own conclusion.
