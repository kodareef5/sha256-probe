# W2-PC2 — Polynomial-Calculus degree from the two-condition structure   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** Each held sr-round adjoins TWO algebraically independent ideal generators (g1=0, h=0); PC refutation **degree** grows **slope-2 per round**, with a bump at 61. The dual of PC1's width.

**Probe run:** Three exact GF(2) measurements at N=3,4,5,6 (throttled). (A) #independent conditions adjoined per held round + their ANF degree (Möbius transform of the gap-algebra g1/h conditions over their local free-word bits). (B) EXACT ANF degree of the *accumulated* collision constraint (degree of the generating set of the ideal "collides through round R") vs enforced tail depth R=16..24, via truth-table→ANF over the N free input bits. (C) TRUE degree-bounded PC/PCR refutation degree on a genuinely-UNSAT mini-SHA (full 8-register collision, diagonal u=v excluded), Macaulay-matrix "is 1 in the GF(2) row-space at degree d" — exactly the card's degree-bounded simulation, never run to completion.

**Result (numbers):**
- **(A) Two generators/round CONFIRMED** — each held round adjoins exactly 2 independent N-bit conditions (g1=0 ∧ h=0). Their individual ANF degrees are small and N-dependent (deg g1 = 1–3, deg h = 3–5), NOT slope-2.
- **(B) Accumulated degree is FLAT** — saturates immediately at the variable bound and stays there: degree **4 for all rounds at N=4**, **5 for all rounds at N=5**; per-round increment = **[0,0,0,0,0,0,0]**. No slope-2, no slope-1, no jump at any round. (An N-bit modular function's ANF degree is capped at N; enforcing more rounds can't push past it.)
- **(C) Refutation degree = 1** at every UNSAT depth (N=3 and N=4, R=16..20): a degree-1 (linear) refutation exists by inspection.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if PC degree is FLAT across k, jumps by 1 not ~2, or shows no discontinuity at 61." — **fired? YES (two clauses).** Accumulated degree is flat (increment 0, not +2) AND shows no discontinuity at any round.

**Verdict reasoning:** KILLED. The card stakes itself on PC *degree* growing slope-2 per round. The degree does not grow at all — it saturates at the variable bound N and is flat across every enforced round (B), and the genuine UNSAT instances admit a degree-1 refutation (C). The card *correctly* identifies that 2 independent generators are adjoined per round (A) — the verified rank-2 / 2^-2N structure (finding #3) — but that rank-2 fact lives in the *generator count*, not in the *refutation degree*: adding two more independent low-degree (≤N) generators per round raises codimension, not degree. So the card's leap from "two conditions" to "degree grows slope-2" is exactly the finding-#3 trap (statistical/ideal independence ≠ a degree lower bound — its own skeptic note: "carries are sparse low-degree XOR layers which could keep degree low"). Per finding #4, there is no discontinuity at 61 (or anywhere): the per-round picture is uniform.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** Honest counterweight — at tiny N (≤6) the ANF degree is bounded by the number of free variables, so (B)'s flatness could in principle be a small-N artifact masking growth that only appears when #free-vars ≫ degree. But the card's *mechanism* is "+2 per round from the two generators," and (A) shows those generators are individually degree ≤5 and mutually low-degree-independent, with no syzygy structure forcing the *refutation* degree up — the degree-bounded refutation closes at d=1. A real PC-degree lower bound needs expansion (Alekhnovich–Razborov), which W2-PC1 just showed is absent (boundary expansion flat, no jump at 61). The two negatives reinforce: the obstruction is the carry nonlinearity (finding #1), not a climbing algebraic degree. To rescue any degree story one would need an N≥16 instance where #free-vars ≫ N AND a measured nonzero per-round degree increment — the larger probe that would decide it; this cheap one decides against.
