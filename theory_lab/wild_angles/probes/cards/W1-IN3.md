# W1-IN3 — Algorithmic mutual information (basis-fixed) → an order parameter for the frontier   ·   VERDICT: SURVIVES

**Card claim:** The colliding pair has a short joint description (the cascade emits M' from M + k correction words). The resource-bounded joint-ARX-circuit size should be O(1) words SHORTER at sr=60 (cascade compresses) and BLOW UP to ≈2|M| at sr=61 (no compression → 2^{-2N}); k(sr) jumps discontinuously at the frontier with nothing shorter below.

**Probe run:** Built the genuine N-bit reduced cascade (`transfer_operator._make_round`). Per round r, measured the **correction-word cost** `cost_words(r) = -log2 P(de(r)=0 | de(r-1)=0)/N`, distinguishing FREE rounds 57-60 (ask: can a correction word zero de? → absorbed, cost≈0) from DETERMINED rounds ≥61 (word fixed by recurrence → residual conditions paid). Exact per-state enumeration of the governing word at N=4,6 (300/120 conditioning states). Cross-checked the **2-condition** structure (g1=0 AND h=0) against the repo ground truth, and ran a shorter-circuit / H(M'|M) brute test at N=4 (R=8,10,12). Throttled.

**Result (numbers):**
- **Sharp discontinuity at the 60→61 frontier — NOT smooth growth.** cost_words(r): rounds 57,58,59,60 = **0.000** each (solvable fraction 1.0 — a correction word always zeroes de); round 61 jumps to **0.94 words (N=4) / 1.11 words (N=6)**, 62 → 1.23/1.11, 63 → 1.02/0.94. The JUMP at 60→61 is **+0.94 / +1.11 words**; max single-step change across 57..63 = 0.94/1.11 (concentrated entirely at the frontier).
- **Two-condition structure confirmed in direction.** A single de=0 gate costs exactly `-log2 P/N = 1.00 word` (N=6); adding the independent inter-message value-match (the h-condition) compounds to P(de=0 AND h-match) ≈ 1.6e-4 ≈ `2^{-2N}` — i.e. each enforced determined round = **2 independent N-bit conditions**, matching `RESULT_sr61_is_2minus2N` (2^{-2N} per enforced round).
- **No shorter circuit found (clause B not fired).** Over 2 free message words at R=8/10/12, the *only* full-output collision with fixed M is M'=M (mean collisions/M = 1.00 ⇒ H(M'|M)=0 words). The probe exposes **no slack** below the cascade's correction budget; it also cannot positively certify minimality at this N.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if k(sr) grows SMOOTHLY across the frontier, OR a ≥2-word-shorter circuit is routinely found." — **fired? no** (neither clause).

**Verdict reasoning:** Kill clause A is the decidable one and clearly does NOT fire: the correction-word cost is flat-zero for the free rounds and jumps sharply at the first determined round (60→61) — a genuine discontinuous order parameter, exactly as the card predicts, and aligned with the repo's 2-condition / 2^{-2N} law. Clause B does not fire either: no ≥2-word-shorter joint circuit was found (the only same-M collision is trivial). It is **SURVIVES** rather than CONFIRMED because (i) my per-state model pins the jump qualitatively (0 → ~1 word per condition, →2 jointly) but does not give a crisp exact "k jumps by exactly 2 words" at reachable N, and (ii) clause B is genuinely undecidable here (min-circuit-size is NP-hard; the cheap ensemble has no nontrivial joint distribution to compress). Promote to a deeper probe.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The card's own skeptic ("only the cascade *upper bound* is certain; can't prove minimality") is realized — the shorter-circuit clause is untestable at this N, so SURVIVES must not be inflated to CONFIRMED. The jump's *location* (60→61) and *absorbed-vs-paid* dichotomy are robust across N=4,6 and reproduce the independently-measured 2^{-2N} step, which is real corroboration. What would settle it: an exact joint-circuit minimization at N=4 over a difference-seeded ensemble with many collisions (needs ≥4 free words so the joint distribution is non-degenerate), measuring H(M'|M) in words and confirming it equals the cascade budget below the frontier and 2|M| above it. Skeptic caveat against over-confidence: the "jump" is partly built into the free-vs-determined word accounting (rounds 57-60 are free by construction); the non-trivial content is that the determined-round gate is genuinely 2 independent conditions, which the data supports.
