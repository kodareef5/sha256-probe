# W3-CA4 — Coequalizer / kernel pair; epi–mono factorization localizes collisions to the ADD   ·   VERDICT: SURVIVES (P-owns-0% holds, but it relabels Davies–Meyer — no new content, per the catalog's borderline flag)

**Card claim:** Collisions = off-diagonal kernel-pair of f; the regular epi–mono factorization f = ADD∘P forces P (invertible) to own 0% of collisions and the ADD 100% — a categorical theorem that "collisions are born in the feed-forward."

**Probe run (N=4,6,8, throttled):** Realized f = ADD∘P as the standard ARX split: **P** = the GF(2) round skeleton (rotations+XOR, all modular carries and Ch/Maj dropped = the `linround` surrogate, composed over 8/16 rounds) — an F₂-linear map, hence a bijection iff full rank; **ADD** = the modular-carry/feed-forward layer P drops. Tests: (R1) is the composed skeleton P a bijection (kernel-pair = diagonal)? (R3) localization — project the output to register a so birthday collisions are abundant, and measure what fraction of full-f colliding pairs are NOT collisions of the carry-free P (carry-born); (R2) the collision-count growth slope vs the pinned 0.74.

**Result (numbers):**
- **(R1) P is a bijection at every N and round count:** rank(P) = 32/32 (N=4), 48/48 (N=6), 64/64 (N=8), corank **0** at both 8 and 16 rounds ⇒ **kernel-pair(P) = diagonal**. At the full-state level this means **P owns 0%** of collisions and the feed-forward ADD owns 100% — exactly the card's theorem.
- **(R3) Localization corroborated:** of the full-f reg-a colliding pairs, the carry-born fraction (pairs that are NOT P-collisions) = **0.947 → 0.985 → 0.996** for N=4,6,8 — rising toward 1. Collisions are overwhelmingly carry-born.
- **(R2) Slope in the known band:** sr=60 cascade collisions 260@N=8 → 946@N=10 give log₂-slope **0.932**; pinned GROWTH_EXPONENT 0.74 (finding #2: not sharp, slope 0.673, spread 0.72–1.04). The slope is the collision-count growth — definitional.

**Kill_criterion:** "P's kernel pair non-trivial, OR slope misses." — **fired? no.** P is a clean bijection (kernel-pair = diagonal) at all N; the slope sits inside the known 0.72–1.04 spread.

**Verdict reasoning:** SURVIVES, **not** CONFIRMED — and this is the rename call (finding #4) the catalog itself pre-flagged ("borderline relabel"). The falsifiable content, P-owns-0%, genuinely holds: the round skeleton is an F₂-bijection (corank 0), so 100% of full-state collisions are born in the feed-forward modular ADD, and the projected-register stress test agrees (carry-born → 0.996). But this is a faithful **restatement of the Davies–Meyer structure**, not new predictive/computational content: rotations and XOR are obviously invertible and the 2n→n feed-forward add is obviously the only non-injective ingredient, so "collisions are born in the ADD" is true-by-construction once you name P the invertible part. The epi–mono factorization computes nothing the repo didn't already have — it does not derive the 0.74 (it re-reads it), does not predict *which* collisions, does not quantify the carry layer. Per finding #4 (RENAME vs MECHANISM), the categorical theorem relabels "carries cause collisions" in coequalizer language without adding leverage. So it survives its kill cleanly but is a relabel, not a CONFIRMED mechanism.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The bijection result is basis-independent and stable (corank 0 at N=4,6,8 × {8,16} rounds), matching prior #1's expectation that a *real* linear object lands on 0/full-rank — here that lands in the card's FAVOR (P invertible), which is the honest read: P really is the mono. Honest caveat on R3: the *projected* skeleton P (output→reg a) is itself heavily non-injective (it is a linear 2n→n map), so "P owns 0%" is a statement about the FULL-state P (R1), not the projection; the projection only shows the *specific* full-f pairs are carry-distinguished (0.95–0.996), which corroborates but is not the same 100%. What would upgrade to CONFIRMED: a coequalizer construction that *computes* a collision invariant unavailable from the raw carry picture (e.g. predicts the 0.74 from the ADD's coequalizer structure, or characterizes the kernel-pair fibres) — none is delivered; the card stays a clean restatement of Davies–Meyer non-injectivity.
