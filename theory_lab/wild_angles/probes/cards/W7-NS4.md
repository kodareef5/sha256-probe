# W7-NS4 — Overspill: is the wall algebra (sharp round) or counting (no sharp round)?   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** Underspill — an internal round-property's first failure is a *standard* round (a sharp algebraic cliff). If a BOOLEAN property flips at exactly 61 uniformly, 61 is a real provable obstruction; if only COUNTS shrink at 61 (no boolean flip), the wall is external/measure-only → no UNSAT proof exists.

**Probe run:** N=6,8,10,12,14 on the repo's faithful `make_helpers(N)` cascade (READ-ONLY). Located the first-breaking round of three internal properties — P1(da_r≡0), P2(de_r single-valued), P3(unique-w-solution) — over 4000 cascade prefixes per N. Throttled: yes.

**Result (numbers):**
- **P1 (da_r=0):** holds at rounds 57–60 for all N (cascade construction forces it), first nonzero at **round 61 for every N** — but da_61 = de_61 (Thm 4), so this is the cascade *ending* (free words exhausted), the same boundary seen from the a-register, not an independent flip.
- **P2 (|de_r image|):** the only genuine boolean flip (single-valued → multi-valued) is at **round 58** uniformly for all N (|de58| = 8,8,16,512,32 > 1; |de57|=|de59|=|de60|=1). **Nothing new flips at 61** — at 61 de just inherits the spread (628, 1899, 3213 …).
- **P3 (# free-W solutions zeroing da_{r+1}):** = **1** (unique) at rounds 57,58,59,60 for all N, then drops to **0 at round 61** because W[61] is schedule-pinned (the schedule recurrence fixes it from W[57..60]+earlier; no free variable remains). A pure degrees-of-freedom / count change.

**Kill_criterion:** "every internal property breaks at a *known* N-uniform round (e.g. de58 at 58) with nothing new flipping at 61 → reproduces known facts, subsumed by NS1/NS2" — **fired? yes.** de's boolean flip is at 58; P1's "break" at 61 is the cascade running out of free words (P3: 1→0), not a new algebraic property.

**Verdict reasoning:** Overspill adjudicates rarity-vs-impossibility, and it comes down on **counting (NO sharp round)**, exactly as prior finding #6 anticipated. The only boolean property that genuinely flips — de single-valuedness — flips at round **58**, a known N-uniform round, not 61. At round 61 *nothing new flips*: the cascade's forced da=0 simply ends because the schedule pins W[61] (P3 solution-count 1→0), so the single change at 61 is a degrees-of-freedom/count drop, not an algebraic cliff. This is the underspill signature of an *external* wall: a measure/count shrinks with no internal boolean obstruction appearing. The round function is identical every round, so there is no algebraic reason 61 differs except that the free-word budget is exhausted there — a bookkeeping boundary. KILLED as a sharp-round claim; the honest answer is no sharp round (consistent with "no round-60/61 knee").

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The P3=0-at-61 entry is structural (the message-schedule recurrence determines W[61]; this is the same fact the repo's `backward_construct` encodes as "W[61] from message schedule, fixed by w59"), not Monte-Carlo measured — but it is the *actual* mechanism, not an assumption: there is no 17th free message word at the tail. P1's uniform "break at 61" could be misread as a sharp cliff, but P2/P3 disambiguate it: da_61=de_61 means it is the *same* boundary as the (counting) cascade terminus, and the only thing that changes at 61 is the free-DOF count. A defender would need a NEW boolean property that is true ≤60 and false at 61 uniformly across N and is *not* reducible to "ran out of free words"; this probe found none. Convergence with NS1 (clean +2N rate step) and CG3 (each enforced round = two 2^-N counting conditions) corroborates the counting reading.
