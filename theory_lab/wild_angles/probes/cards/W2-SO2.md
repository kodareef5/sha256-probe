# W2-SO2 — Quasispecies error threshold: the plateau as an information-maintenance limit   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** Fitness f(m)=exp(−β·HW), per-bit mutation μ. Below μ_c the population localizes around low-HW masters (can climb); above μ_c it delocalizes (HW→N/2). The HW~74 plateau = the system pinned AT its Eigen error threshold; gives μ_c(N) and the optimal solver schedule (sit just below μ_c).

**Probe run:** Reused SO1's exact cascade output-HW machinery (same N=10 M0; fitness = exp(−β·HW(output_diff(tail))) over the 4N=40-bit tail genotype). (a) Wright–Fisher population (pop=250, 100 gens, β=1.0): swept μ from 0.005→0.5, recorded the stationary mean population HW. (b) Built the coarse-grained Eigen HW-shell replication–mutation matrix from the SO1 neighbor census (per-shell down/up/neutral single-flip fractions → mutation kernel; f(h)=exp(−βh) selection), diagonalized, read the dominant eigenvector's mean-HW and IPR vs μ. Throttled (OMP=2, taskpolicy -b), ~2 min.

**Result (numbers):**
- **(a) μ-sweep is a SMOOTH ramp, not a sharp threshold.** Stationary mean HW: 19.0 (μ=0.005) → 20.9 → 22.4 → 26.0 → 29.1 → 29.8 → 30.0 (μ=0.5). The **largest** single-step jump over a μ-doubling is only **3.63 HW** (at μ≈0.04); the curve interpolates gradually to the delocalized plateau (random-tail mean HW = **29.95**). No near-discontinuity = no error catastrophe.
- **The localized phase never sits at the plateau, and never at HW=0 either** in WF: at the lowest μ the population settles at HW≈19 (selection–mutation balance), an intermediate value, then smoothly rises to the entropic mode (30) — it is never "pinned at ~74" by a threshold.
- **(b) The Eigen shell matrix localizes at HW=0 (IPR=1.0) for ALL μ ∈ [10⁻⁴, 0.3].** ⟨HW⟩_dom = 0.00 throughout — in the idealized shell model the HW=0 master always dominates, i.e. collisions are *trivially easy to localize*. There is no delocalization transition; the dominant eigenvector never delocalizes to the plateau at any μ>0.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if the μ-sweep shows a smooth crossover (no sharp threshold), OR the localized phase concentrates at HW≈0 (collisions easy to localize), OR the dominant eigenvector is delocalized for all μ>0." — **fired? YES — clauses 1 and 2 both fire.**

**Verdict reasoning:** KILLED on two independent clauses. (1) The WF μ-sweep is a smooth crossover (max jump 3.6 HW over a 2× change in μ; the whole 19→30 transit is a gradual ramp), not the near-step error catastrophe the card needs. (2) The coarse Eigen shell-matrix's localized phase sits at HW=0 (IPR=1.0 for every μ tested) — i.e. the master is the collision, "easy to localize", the explicit second kill clause. The two probes *disagree on where the localized phase lives* (WF: HW≈19; shell-Eigen: HW=0), and that disagreement is itself the tell: there is no robust localized cloud "at the plateau" that both methods see; the plateau (HW~30) is simply the μ→large **delocalized Binomial mode** — identical to SO1's finding — approached smoothly, not a threshold pinning point.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The card's own skeptic — "SHA's HW-vs-message may be rugged/multi-peak → glassy freezing, not a clean error catastrophe; the master is a measure-zero needle" — is realized: the fitness landscape is SO1's Binomial bowl whose HW=0 master is a 2⁻⁴⁰ needle the finite WF population (pop=250) never finds, so WF localizes at the selection–mutation-balance shell (HW≈19), while the coarse shell-Eigen model (which *grants* HW=0 a reachable shell) collapses onto it for all μ. Neither exhibits the predicted sharp μ_c with the localized phase *at the plateau*. **Answer to the lead's framing: SO2 RENAMES the plateau (= high-μ delocalized Binomial mode = SO1's result); it adds no error-threshold mechanism** — there is no finite μ_c, and "plateau = error catastrophe" does not hold. Convergence with SO1 (both land on the Binomial mode, neither finds a bottleneck/threshold) is corroboration, not coincidence.
