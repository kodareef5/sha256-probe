# W2-NT1 — Collision singular series → derives 2^0.74N as product of local densities   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** R(N) = Σ_{M,M'} Π_r 1[Δ_r≡0]; expand each indicator in additive characters → major arcs give a singular series 𝔖 = Π_r(local density) as the main term, minor arcs negligible by square-root cancellation, and the 0.74 exponent is forced by which rounds give a full vs partial factor of 2^N. Probe: enumerate exact R(N) at N=6,8; compute per-round survival f_r = #{Δ_r≡0}/#{Δ_{r-1}≡0}; does Σ_r log₂ f_r / N → 0.74? Separately confirm minor-arc mass is O(2^{-N/2}).

**Probe run:** N=4,8 (N=6 has no cascade-eligible M0 under the all-ones fill; N=10 count pending, non-load-bearing), throttled. Got exact MSB-cascade sr=60 counts R(N) from `gap_analysis.c` (lab-side binaries, run in `/tmp`): R(4)=49, R(8)=260. Measured per-round single-word survival fractions f_r and formed the Euler-product prediction (2^N)⁴·Πf_r to compare against true R(N). Tested square-root cancellation via the round-58 diff-phase character sum over all nonzero frequencies. Overlaid the repo's own best-kernel scaling data (N=4–11, `paper_figures_data.md` Fig 2 — the actual source of "0.74") to expose the exponent spread.

**Result (numbers):**
- **No Euler product:** de57/de58/de59/de60 are each a single *constant* given the earlier words (#distinct de_r = 1/1/1/1), so de_r=0 is **not reachable by a single free word** — collisions require coordinated multi-word choice. The Euler-product prediction overshoots true R(N) by **+10.4 bits @N=4** (16.0 vs 5.6) and **+24.0 bits @N=8** (32.0 vs 8.0). The rounds are coupled; the product-of-local-densities factorization does not exist.
- **No square-root cancellation:** the round-58 diff phase is *constant* over a single word → |character sum| = **1.000** (full coherence) vs the required 2^{-N/2} ≈ 0.06 (N=8) / 0.03 (N=10). The minor-arc premise fails on this object.
- **0.74 is not sharp (weapon #2):** reproduced the repo best-kernel fit **log₂ C = 0.673·N + 4.76** — exactly the 0.673 refit value, not 0.74. Per-N best-kernel exponents log₂C/N span **1.04 – 2.00** (range 0.96); this MSB-cascade slope = 0.602, per-N exponents 1.00 – 1.40. |0.673 − 0.74| = 0.067.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if Σ log₂ f_r/N ≠ 0.74 (rounds too coupled for an Euler-product factorization)." — **fired? yes.**

**Verdict reasoning:** All three legs of the card collapse. (1) The central claim — R(N) factorizes as a product of per-round local densities — is false: the de_r=0 conditions are each a single constant per fixed prefix, so the surviving collisions arise from coordinated multi-word choices, and the would-be Euler product mispredicts R(N) by 10–24 bits. This is precisely the "rounds too coupled" failure the kill names. (2) The "minor arcs negligible by square-root cancellation" premise fails because the round-58 diff phase is coherent (|S|=1), not √-cancelling. (3) The headline 0.74 is not a sharp derivable main term: the repo's own best-kernel exponent fits to 0.673 with a per-class spread of nearly a full unit (1.04–2.00), so any "derivation landing near 0.74" is meaningful only to ±0.1 and proves nothing (weapon #2). The empirical growth is a noisy global property, not an arithmetic singular series.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The probe *reproduces the repo's published 0.673 slope to three digits* from the independent N=4–11 best-kernel table, which both validates the reproduction and is itself the kill (the "0.74" is a rounded global fit). The card's own skeptic line anticipates this ("SHA rounds are coupled… 0.74 may be a global eigen-property with no Euler product") — and the coupling is exactly what the multi-word-coordination finding demonstrates. What could (weakly) revive a circle-method framing: a genuinely 2-D singular series over *pairs* of rounds that absorbs the coupling — but that is no longer a Π_r product of per-round local densities, so it would not be this card. N=10 (pending) would add a third MSB-cascade point but cannot rescue an Euler product that mispredicts by 24 bits at N=8.
