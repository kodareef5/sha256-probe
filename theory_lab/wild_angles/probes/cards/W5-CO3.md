# W5-CO3 — Up-to-context bisimulation: message modification, unsound at 61   ·   VERDICT: SURVIVES (quantitatively, but the wall = schedule, not round 61)

**Card claim:** Wang message modification IS bisimulation-up-to-context (the free word = the "context" re-closing da=0). Sound through 60 (4 free context moves), unsound/inapplicable at 61 (W[61] schedule-fixed → no context move) → the bare relation pays 2^-2N. Probe: up-to-context shrinks the basin ~2^N per free word at 60, zero at 61.

**Probe run:** N=4 exhaustive BFS of the da=0 cascade relation, round by round. Measured (i) per-round multiplicative growth of the bare bisimulation relation; (ii) adversarial: the as-free context multiplicity of *every* round 57–61; (iii) adversarial: soundness — how many tail points the closure over-certifies if applied at round 61. Throttled.

**Result (numbers):**
- Bare-relation growth per free round (57,58,59,60) = **16.0, 16.0, 16.0, 16.0 = exactly 2^N**. Round 61 (schedule-fixed word) growth = **1.000**. Discontinuity ratio = **16 = 2^N**. The quantitative 2^N-per-context-move prediction holds *exactly*, and the schedule-fixed round contributes no context move — matching the card.
- **Adversarial #1 (per prior finding #4):** if round 61 is *given* a free word, its context multiplicity is **also exactly 16 = 2^N** — identical to rounds 57–60. So round 61 is **not intrinsically different**; it is simply the first round whose word the message schedule fixes (the sigma1-recurrence reaching back to free words ends at 60).
- **Adversarial #2 (soundness):** applying the up-to-context closure *at* round 61 (pretending W[61] is a free move) certifies the da-cascade for **65536/65536** tail points, but only **49** truly collide → the closure is genuinely **unsound at 61** (~1337× over-certification at N=4). The unsoundness is real.

**Kill_criterion:** "same shrinkage at 61 as 60, or none even at 60." — **fired? no.** There IS shrinkage at 60 (exactly 2^N), and the *realized* shrinkage at 61 (schedule-fixed) is 1, different from 60. And the closure is demonstrably unsound exactly where the card says (round 61). The literal discriminator the skeptic demanded — the 2^N-per-word quantitative factor — is met to the digit.

**Verdict reasoning:** SURVIVES on its own kill criterion: the up-to-context reading is *quantitatively exact* (2^N per free context move; sound through 60; unsound at 61 with measured 1337× over-certification). BUT — applying the prior-#4 standard honestly — the round-61 "wall" is **not** an intrinsic property of round 61, the carry, or a bisimulation-specific soundness boundary: round 61 given a free word behaves identically to round 60 (2^N). The discontinuity is entirely the **message schedule** turning W[61] from free to fixed, which is the already-known 2^-2N schedule constraint (g1=0 ∧ h=0, prior finding #3) re-described in Sangiorgi up-to-context language. So it is "real AT 61" only in the trivial sense that 61 is the first fixed-word round — the round function itself is identical every round, consistent with "no round-60 knee."

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** The skeptic warned "free word helps is near-tautological — the 2^N-per-word quantitative prediction is the discriminator." That prediction is met (good for SURVIVES), but adversarial #1 shows the *same* 2^N applies if 61 is freed — so the 2^N factor confirms the up-to-context bookkeeping is correct without making round 61 special. Net: a faithful, quantitatively-confirmed *re-description* of the sr=60/61 boundary in coalgebraic terms — it gives the right number (2^N per word, 2^-2N at the wall) but no new mechanism beyond the schedule constraint. Promotable only if one wants the coalgebraic vocabulary; not an independent lever. (Should NOT be CONFIRMED — that would overclaim a round-61-intrinsic phenomenon the data refutes.)
