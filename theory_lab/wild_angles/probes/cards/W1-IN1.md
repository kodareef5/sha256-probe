# W1-IN1 — Feed-forward coincidence operator → quotient out the bijection   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

**Card claim:** Quotient out the invertible permutation P; define Φ(M)=P(IV,M)⊟IV per lane. Since "the only non-injective op is the final 8×32 modular add," a collision = two inputs in the same coset of the add-kernel; study the adder fibers. Probe predicts the per-lane PRE-ADD modular differences over colliding pairs concentrate at LOW 2-adic valuation (low carry depth).

**Probe run:** Genuine N-bit reduced compression (`transfer_operator._make_round`, scaled rotations), 2 free message words (14 frozen), R rounds. Grouped all 2^{2N} messages by a small kept-lane signature (1–2 lanes = the coincidence/fiber condition); over each fiber's merged pairs, collected the residual per-lane modular differences `s(M)[lane] ⊟ s(M')[lane]` in the other lanes; histogrammed their 2-adic valuation v2 and computed KL vs the uniform-random-nonzero law `P(v2=k)=2^{-(k+1)}`. N=4,6,8; R=10,14,20. **Cross-check:** computed v2 distributions of the repo's *measured* sr-boundary residuals (g1,g2,h) over the 946 real N=10 collisions in `gap_rows.csv`. Throttled.

**Result (numbers):**
- **Residual modular differences are uniform random N-bit values.** v2 histogram matches `2^{-(k+1)}` to 3 decimals at every N and every R: e.g. N=8 obs P(v2=k)=0.502, 0.251, 0.126, 0.063, 0.031,… vs uniform 0.502, 0.251, 0.125, 0.063, 0.031,…. **Max KL across all (N, k_keep, R) = 0.0017 bits** (kill threshold 0.02). Mean v2 equals the uniform mean exactly (0.733 / 0.905 / 0.969 at N=4/6/8).
- **Zero of all cases** show the predicted LOW-valuation concentration. Shallow rounds (R=10, R=14) give the *same* uniform result (KL=0.0000) — it is not an over-diffusion artifact.
- **Independent corroboration on measured data:** the repo's real N=10 sr-boundary gaps g1, g2, h have v2 distributions matching uniform to **KL = 0.0053 / 0.0055 / 0.0073 bits** — again below 0.02. The actual gating residuals are 2-adically uniform too.

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if the pre-add differences are uniform (KL<0.02 bits) — the quotient buys nothing." — **fired? yes**

**Verdict reasoning:** The card's load-bearing prediction (low-2-adic-valuation concentration of pre-add differences) is contradicted: the differences are statistically indistinguishable from uniform random N-bit values (KL ≤ 0.0017 in the probe, ≤ 0.0073 on measured collision data), with zero low-valuation cases. The quotient buys nothing — KILLED, as stated. Found a *second*, structural reason it is dead: for the Davies–Meyer feed-forward `H[lane]=state64[lane] ⊞ IV[lane]`, IV is a COMMON constant, so per-lane modular differences are **identical before and after** the add (`(x+IV)−(y+IV)=x−y`) — the final adder is a per-lane bijection that merges *nothing*. The premise "all non-injectivity lives in the final add" is false; all the many-to-one-ness is in the rounds (M→state_R), which the card explicitly declines to touch.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** A clean negative; not softened. Adversarial self-checks all reinforce it: (a) shallow rounds (R=10/14), where carry/2-adic structure should be most visible, give the same uniform law; (b) two independent fiber conditions (keep 1 lane vs 2) agree; (c) the repo's *measured* sr=61 boundary residuals — a completely independent object from a SAT/enumeration pipeline — are also 2-adically uniform. The only way to rescue the card would be to find a *non-uniform* fiber under some other quotient, but the structural fact (common-IV add preserves modular difference exactly) means there is no "one non-injective adder" to isolate. This matches the Wave-1 spine: the real obstruction is the carry mixing distributed across rounds, not a single isolable algebraic fiber.
