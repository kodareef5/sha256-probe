# W1-PH4 — Feed-forward as Jarzynski ratchet (localization sub-claim)   ·   VERDICT: SURVIVES

**Card claim:** P (the 64-round map) is a bijection → zero entropy production; the feed-forward / non-invertible final add is the *unique* dissipative step, so ALL collision cost is localized there. The cheap yes/no: is the feed-forward-only difference-closure rate 2^-2N, and is P-without-the-final-add rate-flat?

**Probe run:** N=8,10 on the mini-SHA tail (sr regime; mini-SHA reused from W1-PH3). (A) Tested injectivity of the free-tail permutation W[57]→state-after-r57 over all 2^N inputs. (B) Read the feed-forward closure rate (de-closure across the held round) over the full de61=0 hit population via the repo enumerator (N=8). Localization test: replaced the modular adds in the tail by XOR ("reversible add") and remeasured the de61=0 closure rate (40960 samples). Throttled.

**Result (numbers):**
- **(A) P is rate-flat:** the free-tail map is **injective** — distinct=256/256 (N=8) and 1024/1024 (N=10), max fiber=1, **0 colliding pairs**. The final feed-forward add of IV is a per-word translation (bijection), so it cannot add a floor. P contributes a flat (rate-1) factor. ✓
- **(B) closure rate = 2^-2N:** P(both)=P(sr61)=1.419e-05 vs 2^-2N=1.526e-05; **−log2(rate)/N = 2.013** (target 2.0). The two closure conditions (g1=0, h=0) are independent (ratio 0.923). ✓
- **Localization-at-the-irreversible-add: NOT distinguished.** With proper statistics the modular-add and XOR-add single-condition closure rates are **equal**: p_mod=0.00103 (42 hits), p_xor=0.00105 (43 hits), both = 2^-N. (An earlier 6× gap was pure low-statistics noise — 1 vs 6 hits in 4000 samples — and is retracted.)

**Kill_criterion:** "Dead if feed-forward-only closure rate ≠ 2^-2N (floor not localized at the add)." — **fired? no** (the rate is 2^-2N).

**Verdict reasoning:** The card's *literal* probe both pass: the closure rate is 2^-2N (−log2/N=2.013) and the permutation part is rate-flat (injective, zero colliding pairs). So the kill does not fire. But I down-rank from CONFIRMED to **SURVIVES** for two honest reasons: (1) the 2^-2N here is the *same* sr-step object already established in W1-PH1 — PH4 does not produce an independent number, it re-localizes a known one; (2) the part of the claim with actual teeth — "the floor is born at the **non-invertible** add" — is **not** supported: replacing the modular add with reversible XOR leaves the per-condition closure rate unchanged at 2^-N, showing the 2^-N/condition is a *counting* fact (one N-bit schedule equation per condition), not a consequence of the add's irreversibility. So the Jarzynski/localization framing is *consistent* with the data and survives its kill, but the dissipation-localization mechanism is not positively confirmed.

**Cross-check / skeptic note:** This is exactly the card's own skeptic warning: *"Jarzynski on a deterministic Boolean map may give a true-but-vacuous identity; the localization sub-claim is the part with teeth."* My add-vs-XOR control shows the teeth don't bite: irreversibility (carries) is *not* what sets the 2^-N — the same rate survives a fully reversible XOR add. The solid, non-vacuous part is the bijectivity of P (clean, exhaustive). The honest read: "P free, all the rare-event cost at the boundary" is *true as bookkeeping* (P injective; the floor lives in the boundary schedule conditions), but "born at the irreversible add specifically" is false-or-undistinguished. Worth a deeper probe only if one can exhibit a closure rate that *changes* under add→XOR. Corroboration of (A): matches the boundary proof's "P is a permutation in M" and the 132-hard-core picture (the floor is in the boundary conditions, which P merely transports).
