# W4-SH5 — Carry-filtered sheaf → graded H⁰ sheds the overcount, exponent → 0.74   ·   VERDICT: KILLED

CLAUSE B: the growth exponent and whether 0.74 is sharp (established counts {49,260,946,2955} with pair-spread, per prior-finding #2). Throttled (OMP=2, taskpolicy -b).

**Result (numbers):**
- **[A SHRINK] FAILS.** dim ker(Lᵏ) is **FLAT across all carry orders**: **12,12,12,12** (N=3), **16,16,16,16** (N=4), **20,20,20,20** (N=5) for k=0,1,2,N. The filtration does **not** reduce the kernel at all (= R·N at every k), so it cannot shrink toward the true count. Structural reason: each carry layer adds a fresh carry variable **and** exactly one defining relation per bit → **net-zero** change to the collision dofs. A *linear* (GF(2)) carry filtration is incapable of capturing the *nonlinear* carry that actually reduces the count.

**Verdict reasoning:** KILLED.
